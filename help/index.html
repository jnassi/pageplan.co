Common questions

My books aren't syncing between devices

Page Plan syncs through your own iCloud. Make sure you're signed in to iCloud on each device with the same Apple Account. Opening Page Plan picks up changes from your other devices. Settings → iCloud in the app shows when it last synced.

How do I enter an audiobook?

Choose Audiobook as the format, and enter times the way your audiobook app shows them, like 10:32 or 10h 32m. Set your listening speed when you add or edit the book, and Page Plan shows how long each day's listening really takes.

How do I enter an e-book?

Choose E-book. Page Plan uses percent complete, the number your e-reader shows, because page numbers and locations vary from device to device.

How do I set reminders?

Turn on reminders in Settings and pick a time. Tap Add Another Reminder for more, like one in the morning and one in the evening, and swipe one to delete it. Each lists what's left to read today, for every book, and skips you once you've read today's pages.

What's Daily Pace?

The other way to plan a book. When you add or edit it, choose Plan by: Daily pace and enter how much you'll read a day. Page Plan shows when you'll finish, and the date moves up when you read more.

How does the reading goal work?

Turn it on in Settings and choose yearly or monthly, and how many books. It counts the books you mark Finished in that year or month. Tap the goal card on your book list to see them, and tap Share to make an image for Instagram or anywhere else.

Why does the plan finish the day before my due date?

So the meeting isn't your last reading day. To read on the due date too, edit the book and turn on Read on the due date.

I missed a few days. Why does it say I'm behind?

"Behind" is measured against the plan you started with, so you can see what catching up will take. The daily number already includes the catch-up: just read today's pages.

The page count is wrong

Book lookup gives a starting point, but page counts vary by edition. Edit the book and enter the count from your copy.

Is my data private?

Yes. There's no account and no Page Plan server, and your books stay on your devices and in your own iCloud. See the privacy policy.
