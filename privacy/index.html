Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Page Plan is a reading planner for iPhone and iPad, made by Jason Nassi. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: Page Plan doesn't collect any of your data, and I can't see your books.

What Page Plan stores, and where

The books you add, your reading progress, due dates and settings are stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, they also sync to your own private iCloud account so they appear on your other devices. That data lives in Apple's iCloud under your account. I don't have access to it, and there is no Page Plan server.

Book lookup

When you look up a book, Page Plan sends the title or author you typed directly from your device to Google Books, or to Open Library if Google doesn't find it, and downloads the matching details and cover image. Those services receive your search and your device's network address, as any website does. Their own privacy policies apply:

  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Open Library (Internet Archive): https://archive.org/about/terms.php

Page Plan doesn't send anything else to them, and I don't receive or keep your searches.

Reminders

If you turn on the daily reminder, the notification is scheduled on your device. Nothing about it is sent anywhere.

What Page Plan doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-ups
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising
  • No selling or sharing of data

This website

pageplan.co counts visits with GoatCounter, which doesn't use cookies and doesn't store your IP address or anything that identifies you. It records which pages are viewed, which links are clicked, the referring site, and general browser and country information. The site has no ads and no other tracking.

Children

Page Plan is suitable for all ages and doesn't collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
